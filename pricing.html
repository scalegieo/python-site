Billing questions

Straight
answers.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. Send us a link to one of your current listings and we'll cut a short vertical reel from its photos for free. It's yours to post whether or not you hire us.

How do I pay?

Every project is invoiced through Stripe, so you can pay by card or bank transfer. Retainers are billed monthly at the start of each cycle.

What counts as a listing on a retainer?

One property address. Each listing on a retainer gets a full cinematic walkthrough, two social reels and all three formats.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Move between the single listing package and the retainers at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Do unused listings roll over?

Retainer listings reset each month. If you consistently need more, the Brokerage Team package covers up to 10 listings across your team.
