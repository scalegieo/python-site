Why video in Boulder

A market that
scrolls first.

Boulder buyers include relocating tech and research professionals, CU families and second-home buyers from out of state. Many see a listing on a screen long before they see it in person. A cinematic walkthrough gives them the flow of the home, the light and the view in thirty seconds.

For luxury listings that's table stakes. For everything else it's an edge, because most listings in town still launch with photos alone.

Our Boulder lifestyle intro opens your film with the place itself: the Flatirons, Pearl Street, the Chautauqua trails and the Saturday farmers market. It's five seconds that tell a buyer from out of state why they want to live here.
