Host questions

Good
questions.

Will video get me more bookings?

We can't promise a number, and anyone who does is guessing. What a film gives you is shareable content for Instagram, TikTok and your direct-booking site, which is where guests who don't pay platform fees come from.

Can I use the film on Airbnb and VRBO?

Use the stills and the film wherever each platform allows. Most hosts get the most value from the reels on Instagram and on their own booking site.

What's a seasonal refresh?

On the Host Retainer we make a new film each quarter that reflects the season, like snow on the deck in winter or aspens in fall, using updated photos you send us.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. The Host Retainer covers up to three properties, and we can quote larger portfolios.
