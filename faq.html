Getting started

How does it work if you never visit the property?

We work entirely from your existing listing photos. Each photo becomes a cinematic shot with smooth camera motion and natural light, and we cut those shots into one edited film with music and your branding.

What do you need from me?

A link to the listing or 15 to 40 high-resolution photos, plus the address, price and any branding you want on screen. That's it.

Can I really get a free sample?

Yes. Send us the link to one of your current listings and we'll cut a short vertical reel from its photos. It's yours to post, no strings attached.

Do you work outside Boulder?

Yes. We focus on Boulder, the Front Range and Colorado's mountain towns, but because we work from photos we can serve listings anywhere in the US.

Quality & honesty

Is this made with AI?

We use AI-assisted motion tools together with traditional editing and color grading. Every clip is generated from your original photography and reviewed by a human editor, and anything that warps, morphs or invents detail is cut.

Will the video show features that don't exist?

Never. We don't add rooms, pools, views, furniture or finishes that aren't in your photos, and we make no structural or architectural changes. Misrepresenting a property violates real estate advertising rules, and we take that seriously.

Is virtual twilight allowed?

Virtual twilight changes the sky and lighting, not the property. Some MLSs ask that enhanced images be labeled; we recommend following your MLS's rules.

Do I need the rights to the listing photos?

You should have permission to use the listing photography for marketing. Most agents do through their agreement with their photographer. If you're not sure, check with your photographer before sending.

Delivery & formats

How fast will I get my video?

Within 24 hours of receiving your photos. Retainer clients get priority 12-hour delivery, and same-day rush is available on any listing.

What formats do I get?

Every film is delivered in 4K in three shapes: vertical 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, horizontal 16:9 for the MLS and YouTube, and square 1:1 for Facebook.

Can I post it on the MLS?

Yes. The horizontal cut is made for the MLS. If your MLS doesn't allow branding, add the format pack and we'll include an unbranded version.

What about music?

Every film is scored with commercially licensed music suitable for social media. If you have a track you'd like, tell us and we'll check its license.

How many revisions do I get?

Single listings include one round of revisions. Retainer and brokerage clients get unlimited revisions.

Pricing & billing

How much does it cost?

Single listing films are $350. The Agent Retainer is $1,200 a month for up to four listings, and the Brokerage Team package is $2,500 a month for up to ten. Host plans start at $300. See pricing for details.

How do I pay?

We invoice through Stripe, so you can pay by card or bank transfer. Retainers are billed monthly.

Who owns the finished video?

You receive a license to use the finished film to market the property and your business on any platform, for as long as you like.
