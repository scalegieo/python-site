Privacy.

Last updated October 2, 2026.

Python ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you visit our website or work with us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, email, phone number, listing links, photos, branding assets and anything you include in a message.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic usage data: standard server logs and, if enabled, privacy-friendly analytics that tell us which pages are visited.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and deliver the work you ask for.
  • To send invoices and project updates.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we need to run the business, such as file storage, email and payment processing, and only for those purposes.

Embedded video

Some pages embed videos from YouTube. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode where possible, but YouTube may set cookies once you play a video. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Your photos and files

Photos and assets you send us are used only to make your films. We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@pythonwalkthroughs.com.
