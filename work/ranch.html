The brief

Ranch

Acreage is hard to photograph and easy to feel in motion. We open on the land, move through the residence, the water and the arena, and finish on horseback at golden hour. If your set includes aerials, we use them; if it doesn't, we won't invent acres.

Property:
190-acre equestrian estate with a luxury residence.
Shot list:
Meadows, private lake, residence, covered arena, golden hour.
Delivered:
Long horizontal cut for YouTube plus vertical teasers.
Best for:
Land, acreage and horse properties that photos undersell.
