The brief

Golf

Golf sells the setting. For clubs we open wide on the landscape, then fly the signature holes in the order a golfer plays them, built to drive memberships, tournaments and events. For course-side homes we start on the fairway and move back toward the rooms that look out at the grass. If the footage shows the green, the film shows the green. If it doesn't, we don't invent one.

Property:
Coastal golf links with cliff-top and beachside holes.
Shot list:
Clubhouse-to-coast reveal, cliff-top flyover, beach holes, fairway glide.
Delivered:
Hero film plus social reels for memberships, events and real estate.
Best for:
Golf clubs, resort communities and course-side listings.
